Part.07our story

we came
home.

As travel enthusiasts, we, the founders, have drawn inspiration from our café and culinary experiences across the globe. That influence lives in every detail of Neighbourhood—from the carefully curated menu to the thoughtfully designed, minimal yet inviting interiors.

We’re a couple with a shared love for travel, coffee, food and plants. Freshly baked in-house breads and bakery delights are central to what we serve, adding a personal touch to your experience.

After working with startups and MNCs in bustling metro cities, we returned to our hometown, Indore, to create something special—right in your neighbourhood. This is our way of giving back to the city where we were born and where our hearts belong.

We’re proud to collaborate with local artisans and creators, celebrating their contributions by sharing their names and stories in everything they’ve helped us build.

Welcome to Neighbourhood, with all our love—a space to slow down, connect and find your own story.

Aayush & Shefali
